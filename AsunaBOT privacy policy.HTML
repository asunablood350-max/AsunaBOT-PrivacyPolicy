Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AsunaBOT handles information when you use the bot.

1. Information We Collect

AsunaBOT does not store personal user data or server data.

The bot may receive information from Discord that is necessary for its features to function, but this information is not stored by AsunaBOT.

2. Data Storage

AsunaBOT does not maintain a database containing personal user information or server information.

Information processed by the bot is not intentionally stored for later use.

3. Data Sharing

AsunaBOT does not sell, share, or provide stored user or server data to third parties.

4. Discord

AsunaBOT operates through Discord and uses information provided through the Discord platform when necessary for the bot's functionality.

Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own policies and terms.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future if the functionality of AsunaBOT changes or if new privacy requirements apply.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

6. Questions

If you have questions or concerns about AsunaBOT's privacy practices, you can report them through the AsunaBOT support server.
